Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 16, 2025

Welcome to Astra Veil. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our AI-powered astrology app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Email address (via Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In)
  • Birth Information: Birth date, time, and location for astrological calculations
  • Chat History: Conversations with our AI astrologer to provide personalized insights
  • Profile Facts: Information you share during conversations (relationship status, interests, etc.)
  • Device Information: Push notification tokens, app usage analytics
  • Subscription Status: RevenueCat purchase history for entitlement verification

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Generate personalized astrological readings and birth chart analysis
  • Provide AI-powered chat experiences tailored to your cosmic profile
  • Send daily forecast notifications (if enabled)
  • Process and verify subscription purchases
  • Improve our AI models and app experience
  • Communicate important updates about the service

3. AI and Data Processing

Astra Veil uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate astrological insights. When you interact with our AI:

  • Your birth data and conversation history are sent to our secure edge functions
  • Data is processed server-side to generate personalized responses
  • We do not use your data to train third-party AI models
  • All AI processing is done through encrypted connections

4. Data Storage and Security

We take data security seriously:

  • All data is stored in Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS) enabled
  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest
  • API keys and secrets are stored server-side only
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and download your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications
  • Request corrections to your birth information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

6. Data Retention

We retain your data as follows:

  • Account data: Until you delete your account
  • Chat history: 90 days (rolling deletion)
  • Anonymized analytics: Indefinitely for product improvement
  • Subscription records: 7 years (tax/legal requirements)

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication
  • Anthropic: AI model for readings
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • PostHog: Analytics (privacy-focused)
  • Apple/Google: Sign-in authentication

8. Children's Privacy

Astra Veil is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected data from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
