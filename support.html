Frequently Asked

Purchases, subscriptions, and billing

Astra Veil Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID. Manage, cancel, or restore it in iOS Settings → Subscriptions, or use the Restore Purchases option in the app. If a charge looks wrong, email us with your Apple ID and purchase receipt and we'll sort it out.

Reporting a response

Long-press any of Celestia's responses in the app to copy or report it. Reports are reviewed by our team within 48 hours. If you or someone you know is in crisis, the in-app guidance links to free, confidential support: call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line), or call 911 if you are in immediate danger.

My data and privacy

Your birth details and readings are yours. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what we store and why, or email us to export or delete your account at any time.
